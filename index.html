How I build

Every project on this page holds to these. If a feature needs your real name, your location or a tracker to work, it doesn't ship.

  • No ads. Ever.

    No ad networks, no sponsored spots, no selling your attention. No analytics SDKs or telemetry either, including on this page.

  • Collect the minimum

    Handles, not names. No addresses, no phone numbers. If I don't need it, I don't ask for it.

  • Secure from the first line

    Hashed passwords, keys instead of shared secrets, least privilege. Security goes in at the start, not bolted on after launch.

  • Yours to keep

    You can leave with your data, delete your account by asking, and use the free version without being nagged into a paid one.

  • Revenant Poker

    Live

    Real-time multiplayer poker door for BBSes

    No-Limit Hold'em and Pot-Limit Omaha at live tables shared across boards: a caller on Revenant and a caller on Haunted Node sit at the same felt. A hosted hub shuffles with a cryptographically secure deck and decides every hand, so a modified door can't change the outcome. Clean 80x25 ANSI with a four-color deck, hand strength, bet presets and a weekly leaderboard. Press K on Revenant.

    • No ads
    • Play chips only
    • No cash value
    • Hub decides every hand
    How the cards are dealt
    The shuffle

    Every hand starts with a fresh 52-card deck, shuffled on the hub before the first card goes out. The shuffle is Fisher-Yates, which gives every possible order of the deck the same chance. Each step draws from the server's cryptographic random number generator, the same source used to make encryption keys. Nothing about the players, the table or the time goes into it, so the next card can't be predicted.

    The deal works like a real table: cards come off the top in order, with a card burned before the flop, the turn and the river.

    Who sees what

    The deck never leaves the hub. Your door is sent your own cards and the board, and nothing else. You see another player's cards only when they're shown at showdown. A modified door can't peek, because the cards it would need are never sent to it.

    The sysop panel shows players, tables and bankrolls, never cards. A board only receives its own callers' cards, the ones already on their screens, so no sysop can see the deck or the cards of anyone calling from another board.

    House bots play by the same rules. A bot gets its own two cards and the board, works out its odds against random hands, and acts. It never sees your cards or the deck, and it's always labeled "bot" at the table.

    Hand records

    The hub saves the deck order and every action of every hand, so any hand can be replayed card by card. If a hand looks wrong, ask your sysop to send it our way. There is no setting, admin command or door option that picks, holds or changes a card.

    It's a play-money game, built as if it weren't, because a game is only fun when you know it's straight.

    Sysops

    Running a BBS? Ask about getting the door at revenantpoker.com. It works on Mystic, Synchronet and other modern boards.

Connect

Getting people to the boards: finding one that's up, dialing in from a browser, and soon from a real phone line.

  • NodeDial

    Live

    The BBS list. Checked daily.

    A free list of bulletin boards you can call today, by telnet, SSH, the web or a real modem. Every board has to answer a check to get on, and each one is checked about five times a day after that, so you can see what's up before you dial. The order shuffles daily by a public formula anyone can verify. Sysops add or claim their board free, and it loads straight into SyncTERM or NetRunner. The browser dialer behind Revenant and Haunted Node is going open source next.

    • No ads
    • No paid placement
    • Checked 5x a day
    • Free
